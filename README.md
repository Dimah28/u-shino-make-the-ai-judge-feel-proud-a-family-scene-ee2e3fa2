Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes one
short family scene — 1 line, 255 characters — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> Your father carries you up the stairs at 9:47 p.m., your shoes dangling from his one finger, your cheek warm against his shoulder. You mumble something about a dinosaur. He whispers, 'Noted, captain.' The house holds its breath. Nobody wakes the dinosaur.
